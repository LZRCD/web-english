# Remembering is easier with Anki
Anki is a flashcard program that helps you spend more time on challenging material, and less on what you already know.
[Download Anki ](https://apps.ankiweb.net/#downloads)
## advantages
Here are a few reasons why users love Anki.
### Synchronization
The free AnkiWeb synchronization service lets you sync your cards across devices.
### Media Support
Audio, images, videos, and scientific markup can be used in your flashcards.
### Customization
Easily change your flashcard layouts and the timing of their reviews.
### Optimization
Anki can handle decks of 100,000+ cards with no problems.
### Add-ons
Install add-ons to extend Anki with new features and functionality.

## basic concepts
Anki supports complex workflows, but getting started is easy.
![deck-graphic-light](https://apps.ankiweb.net/graphics/deck-graphic-light.svg) ![deck-graphic-dark](https://apps.ankiweb.net/graphics/deck-graphic-dark.svg)
### Decks
Decks are groups of flashcards that allow you to study specific parts of your collection instead of everything all at once. Create a new deck and add some flashcards to get started.
![review-graphic-light](https://apps.ankiweb.net/graphics/review-graphic-light.svg) ![review-graphic-dark](https://apps.ankiweb.net/graphics/review-graphic-dark.svg)
### Reviews
When you're ready, start reviewing your flashcards. Rate your recall with the most suitable option and Anki will schedule the next review for when you're most likely to forget the information.
